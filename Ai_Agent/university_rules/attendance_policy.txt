UNIVERSITY ACADEMIC REGULATIONS AND POLICIES
==============================================

SECTION 1: ATTENDANCE POLICY
-----------------------------

1.1 Minimum Attendance Requirement
All students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in each registered course to be eligible for end-semester examinations.

1.2 Attendance Calculation
- Attendance is calculated from the first day of classes
- Laboratory sessions, tutorials, and lectures are all counted
- Attendance percentage = (Classes Attended / Total Classes Conducted) × 100

1.3 Medical and Emergency Leave
Students who are absent due to medical reasons or family emergencies may submit supporting documentation:
- Medical certificates must be from registered medical practitioners
- Certificates must be submitted within 3 working days of return
- Maximum 2 weeks of medical leave can be accommodated per semester
- Medical leave days are excluded from attendance calculation after verification

1.4 Attendance Shortage Appeals
Students falling below 75% attendance may submit an appeal to the Academic Council if:
- They have documented medical reasons covering significant absence period
- They have maintained good academic standing (CGPA > 6.0)
- They can demonstrate genuine extenuating circumstances

Appeals Process:
- Submit written appeal with supporting documents to Department Head
- Appeal reviewed by Academic Council within 5 working days
- Council may grant conditional examination permission
- Students must attend mandatory makeup classes if assigned

1.5 Consequences of Inadequate Attendance
- 65-74%: Warning issued, counseling recommended
- Below 65%: Not eligible for examinations without approved appeal
- Below 50%: Automatic course withdrawal, must re-register next semester


SECTION 2: GRADING AND EVALUATION POLICY
-----------------------------------------

2.1 Grading Scale
- A+ (90-100): Outstanding
- A (80-89): Excellent
- B+ (70-79): Very Good
- B (60-69): Good
- C (50-59): Satisfactory
- D (40-49): Pass
- F (Below 40): Fail

2.2 Grade Disputes and Re-evaluation
Students who believe their work has been graded incorrectly may request re-evaluation:
- Request must be submitted within 5 days of grade publication
- Request must specify which assignment/exam is being contested
- A fee of ₹500 per assessment is charged (refunded if grade changes)
- Re-evaluation conducted by different faculty member
- Decision of re-evaluation committee is final

2.3 Assignment Grading Standards
All assignments are evaluated based on:
- Content accuracy and depth (40%)
- Organization and structure (20%)
- Critical thinking and analysis (20%) 
- Citations and references (10%)
- Presentation and formatting (10%)

Faculty must provide:
- Detailed rubric before assignment submission
- Feedback within 10 working days
- Comments explaining point deductions

2.4 Appeals Process for Grading
If re-evaluation doesn't resolve the issue:
- Student may appeal to Department Academic Committee
- Appeal must include original work, grading rubric, and detailed explanation
- Committee reviews within 2 weeks
- Committee decision is binding


SECTION 3: LIBRARY AND FACILITIES POLICY
-----------------------------------------

3.1 Library Card Activation
All registered students receive a library card upon enrollment:
- Card activated within 48 hours of registration
- Valid for entire program duration
- Must be renewed each academic year

3.2 Library Card Suspension
Cards may be suspended if:
- Overdue books not returned (>30 days)
- Unpaid fines exceed ₹1000
- Damage to library property reported
- Academic probation status

3.3 Resolving Library Card Issues
If card is not working:
- Check for any pending dues at library counter
- Verify student status is "Active" in student portal
- Contact library administration (library@university.edu)
- Issues resolved within 24 hours of reporting

3.4 Borrowing Privileges
- Undergraduate students: 5 books for 14 days
- Graduate students: 10 books for 30 days
- Faculty: 20 books for 90 days
- Reference books: Library use only, no borrowing


SECTION 4: EXAM AND ASSESSMENT POLICY
--------------------------------------

4.1 Examination Eligibility
To be eligible for examinations:
- Minimum 75% attendance (or approved appeal)
- All fee payments up to date
- No pending disciplinary action
- All required coursework submitted

4.2 Exam Absence Due to Emergency
If student misses exam due to emergency:
- Notify department within 24 hours
- Submit supporting documentation within 3 days
- Makeup exam scheduled within 2 weeks
- Valid reasons: medical emergency, family bereavement, accident

4.3 Academic Integrity
- Plagiarism results in zero marks and possible suspension
- Cheating in exams leads to semester cancellation
- Unauthorized collaboration on individual work is prohibited
- Proper citation required for all sources


SECTION 5: STUDENT CONDUCT AND DISCIPLINE
------------------------------------------

5.1 Expected Conduct
Students must:
- Respect faculty, staff, and fellow students
- Maintain academic integrity
- Follow university rules and regulations
- Dress appropriately for academic environment

5.2 Prohibited Conduct
- Harassment or discrimination of any kind
- Violence or threats
- Substance abuse on campus
- Vandalism or property damage

5.3 Disciplinary Procedures
Violations are handled by:
- Minor infractions: Department level (warning, probation)
- Major violations: Disciplinary Committee (suspension, expulsion)
- Appeals to Vice Chancellor within 10 days


SECTION 6: COMPLAINT RESOLUTION PROCESS
----------------------------------------

6.1 Filing Complaints
Students may file complaints regarding:
- Academic matters (grading, attendance, faculty conduct)
- Administrative issues (fees, records, facilities)
- Harassment or discrimination
- Safety concerns

6.2 Complaint Channels
- Department Head: Academic matters
- Student Affairs Office: Administrative and general issues
- Grievance Cell: Harassment, discrimination, serious concerns
- Anonymous hotline: 1800-XXX-XXXX

6.3 Resolution Timeline
- Acknowledgment: Within 24 hours
- Initial review: Within 3 working days
- Resolution: Within 15 working days for most issues
- Complex cases: Up to 30 days

6.4 Appeal Process
If unsatisfied with resolution:
- Appeal to next higher authority
- Provide written reasons for appeal
- Appeal reviewed within 10 working days


SECTION 7: SPECIAL PROVISIONS
------------------------------

7.1 Students with Disabilities
- Extended exam time (up to 1 hour extra)
- Scribe assistance available
- Accessible course materials
- Relaxed attendance (60% minimum with documentation)

7.2 Sports and Cultural Representatives
- 10% attendance relaxation for university-level participation
- Makeup classes and assignments provided
- Certificate from Sports/Cultural Committee required

7.3 International Students
- Cultural orientation provided
- Academic advisor assigned
- English language support available
- Visa and immigration assistance
